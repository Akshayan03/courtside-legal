Courtside

Pro basketball scores, box scores, standings and stats for iPhone.

Help and Support

Need help, found a problem, or have an idea? Open an issue at github.com/Akshayan03/courtside-legal/issues. Please include your iPhone model, its iOS version and the app version (Following › Settings › About). Don't post anything private.

Scores and games

The Games tab shows a strip of days. Live games show the quarter and clock and refresh about every 15 seconds while you're looking at them. Tap a game for the summary, box score, play-by-play, win probability and shot chart. Pull down on any screen to refresh.

Your team

Pick a team when you first open the app, or later in Following. Followed teams are pinned to the top of every list.

Alerts

Tip-off alerts are scheduled on your iPhone ahead of time, so they arrive even when Courtside is closed. Close-game and final-score alerts depend on iOS waking the app in the background, which iOS does on its own schedule, so they can arrive late. Turn alerts on or off in Following.

Widgets and Live Activities

Add the Courtside widget from the Home Screen (touch and hold › Edit › Add Widget). To choose the team, touch and hold the widget and pick Edit Widget. On a game page, tap the bell to follow the game live on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

No connection?

Courtside keeps the last scores it loaded. When you're offline, or the data service isn't answering, a banner says so and shows when the saved scores are from. Pull down to try again.

About the data

Courtside is an unofficial app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the NBA, its teams or ESPN. Scores, stats and headlines come from ESPN's publicly available data feeds. Team badges in the app are Courtside's own designs, not team logos.

Privacy

No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Read the Privacy Policy.
