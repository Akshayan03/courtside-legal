Courtside

Privacy Policy · effective 30 October 2026

The short version

Courtside collects no personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The teams and players you follow and your alert settings stay on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

The teams and players you follow, your alert choices, which team each widget shows, and saved copies of recently loaded scores and stats (so the app works offline). None of it is sent to the developer or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

When the app goes online

To show scores, Courtside downloads public data from ESPN's servers (site.api.espn.com and related ESPN addresses): scoreboards, game details, standings, team and player stats, and headlines. When you search, the words you type are sent to ESPN's search to find players. ESPN receives these requests and your device's IP address, as any website would; Courtside sends no identifier of you or your device. When you open a news story, it opens ESPN's web page inside the app; ESPN's own privacy policy applies to that page.

Notifications

Alerts are created on your iPhone by the app itself. There is no push server, and no notification token is sent anywhere.

Children

Courtside collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your choices

Unfollow teams or players and turn alerts off in the app at any time, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: see Help and Support.
